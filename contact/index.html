Corrections

If a FedRAMP, DoD or OneGov fact looks wrong, email the page link and what you believe is incorrect. Public-source facts are corrected by re-checking the source; FedCatalog does not edit authorization, agency or spending data by hand.

Vendors

FedCatalog links to a search for your company on each marketplace and vehicle rather than to individual listings, so there is nothing to submit. If your FedRAMP record’s website is wrong or missing, correct it with FedRAMP; FedCatalog picks up the change on its next refresh.
