What it does

CORAS® is an agentic-AI decision intelligence platform developed to meet the operational needs of federal agencies, including the Department of Defense. It provides a secure environment for analyzing complex data, modeling scenarios, and executing workflows within structured decision frameworks. The platform is designed for use in environments that require high accountability, traceability, and compliance. At the center of the platform is Gary, a secure, LLM-powered orchestrator that understands mission context, executes real-time scenarios, and activates purpose-built AI agents to generate insights, automate workflows, and accelerate outcomes.
