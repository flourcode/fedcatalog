What it does

Oracle Cloud Enterprise Performance Management (Oracle Cloud EPM) is a full suite of industry standard EPM business processes comprised of planning (strategy, financial, workforce, capital, projects), budget formulation and execution, consolidation and close, account reconciliation, profitability & cost management, tax reporting, narrative reporting, and enterprise data management. Customers can subscribe to some or all of the EPM Applications. Oracle Cloud EPM is a true SaaS offering, operated and maintained by Oracle within its secure Government Cloud facilities in the United States.
