What it does

Illumio Government Cloud delivers Zero Trust Segmentation, including microsegmentation, across multi-cloud and data center workloads and endpoints for government agencies and departments. This offering includes Illumio Core and Illumio Endpoint. Illumio Core provides Zero Trust Segmentation for cloud and data center workloads. Illumio Core stops ransomware and cyberattacks from spreading by delivering intelligent visibility, radically simple label-based policy creation, and automated segmentation and enforcement.
