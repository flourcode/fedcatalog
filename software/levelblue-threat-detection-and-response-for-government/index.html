What it does

LevelBlue’s Threat Detection and Response for Government (TDR for Gov) is delivered as a SaaS offering using a multi-tenant public cloud computing environment. It is available to federal, state, local, and tribal governments, as well as research institutions, federal and government contractors. TDR for Gov detects, analyzes and may respond to threats and vulnerabilities. It is built on LevelBlue’s Unified Security Management (USM) platform. The service is available as a self-managed service or with 24x7 monitoring by LevelBlue's Security Operations Center, an enhancement of TDR for Gov, (Managed Threat Detection and Response for Government).
