What it does

Kaseware for U.S. Government is delivered as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) offering using a multi-tenant hybrid cloud computing environment. The service is available to U.S. defense and federal civilian agencies, as well as state, local, and tribal government organizations. Kaseware is a cloud-based investigative and case management system designed to support the collection, management, analysis, and secure sharing of investigative, incident, and intelligence-related information.
