What it does

Ivanti Service Manager, powered by HEAT, helps IT teams meet today's government and user demands for effective service delivery and support. The solution, with workflow automation, mobile and integrated Voice support, enables IT teams to quickly deploy and configure a world-class service management solution. Cloud deployment is available from FedRAMP compliant AWS data centers in North America. Service Manager is designed to be the central point of contact between users, employees and the IT organization.
