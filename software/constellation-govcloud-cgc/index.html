What it does

Constellation GovCloud (CGC) is an information system that provides the necessary Platform as a Service (PaaS) architecture layers to support Merlin-operated Software as a Service (SaaS) applications. These Merlin SaaS applications will be hosted and provided to U.S. federal agencies, including local and state governments and tribal organizations. The CGC is part of a managed service that is designed to accelerate FedRAMP authorizations allowing Merlin SaaS companies to achieve a FedRAMP Authority to Operate (ATO) more quickly and cost-effectively.
