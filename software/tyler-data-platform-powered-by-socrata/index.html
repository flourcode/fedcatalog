What it does

The Tyler Data Platform, powered by Socrata®, which includes Enterprise Data Platform and Open Data Platform, is the market leader in making government data discoverable, usable, and actionable for government workers and the people they serve. Tyler Data & Insights provides the data platform and cloud applications exclusively for city, county, state, and federal government organizations. Government programs generate massive amounts of data, but most of it is locked away in hard-to-access data silos.
