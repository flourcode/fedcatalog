What it does

UberEther Advantage The UberEther Advantage platform offers a highly secure and easy-to-deploy boundary platform with FedRAMP High and DoD IL5 authorizations based on NIST 800-53 Revision 5 security control requirements. The platform includes two main solutions within the authorization boundary: • ATO Advantage: An empty boundary platform designed to accelerate FedRAMP and DoD authorization for agency solutions and third-party software products. • IAM Advantage: A comprehensive identity and access management (IAM) solution equipped with pre-installed, configured, and optimized IAM tools.
