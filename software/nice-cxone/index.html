What it does

NiCE CXone is a FedRAMP Moderate cloud contact center platform that empowers U.S. federal, state, local, and tribal governments, research institutions, and contractors to deliver modern, secure, and citizen-centric service experiences. By unifying omnichannel engagement, AI, analytics, and workforce optimization, it helps agencies improve responsiveness, efficiency, and trust. Key Capabilities: Unified Citizen Engagement Platform Streamline operations with a single platform that combines advanced routing, centralized administration, and compliant recording.
