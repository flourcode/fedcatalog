What it does

The Digital Enterprise Suite is a highly visual and interactive software suite that enables technical staff to encapsulate programmatic logic and lets non-technical business people assemble the business logic to innovate, transform and improve. The Digital Enterprise Suite combines intuitive business modeling with flexible, performant and scalable business automation. It accelerates digital transformation, innovation and improvement via visualization from business discovery through to automation. The Digital Enterprise Suite is Composed of the Digital Modeling Suite and the Digital Automation Suite.
