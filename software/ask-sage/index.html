What it does

Ask Sage is a Multi-modal Generative AI platform designed specifically to enable secure access to Generative AI models for both government teams. Ask Sage is built on Generative AI technologies such as Azure OpenAI (GPT 3.5, GPT 4, GPT 4o etc.), AWS Bedrock Gov (LLMA3, Titan etc.), Google Vertex AI (Google Gemini Pro 1.5 etc.), and various open-source LLMs. Ask Sage also supports OpenAI Whisper, enabling Speech to Text capabilities on videos and audio files and DALL-E enabling image generation. The platform's data ingest and training are model-agnostic, ensuring secure training of proprietary data without ingesting it into the models.
