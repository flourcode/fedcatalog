What it does

Appian Government Cloud (AGC) Moderate provides Appian’s process automation platform for moderate-impact workloads. AGC offers Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) and Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) functionality, which empowers agencies to design, automate, and optimize their operations to increase efficiency and productivity.
