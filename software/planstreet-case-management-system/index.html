What it does

PlanStreet is a comprehensive case management platform created to help agencies modernize service delivery, streamline workflows, and ensure accountability in mission-critical programs. With access to real-time data, intelligent automation, and seamless integrations, PlanStreet greatly reduces administrative tasks while improving transparency and performance. Transforming Federal Case Management: ✔ Case Management Services – Centralized, secure case handling for improved service coordination. ✔ Incident Management – Real-time tracking, risk assessment, and resolution for critical incidents. ✔ Investigation Management – Comprehensive tools for audits, compliance, and case integrity. ✔ Grants
