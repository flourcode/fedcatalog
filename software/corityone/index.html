What it does

Cority gives every employee from the field to the board room the power to make a difference, reducing risks and creating a safer, healthier, and more sustainable world. Cority's people-first software solutions were built by EHS and sustainability experts who know the pressures businesses and agencies face. Time-tested, scalable, and configurable, CorityOne consists of a comprehensive suite of solutions for managing environmental, health, safety, sustainability, and quality programs. The responsible business platform combines datasets from across the organization to enable improved efficiencies, actionable insights, data-driven decisions, and more accurate reporting on performance.
