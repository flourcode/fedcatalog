What it does

CLEAR1 is CLEAR’s secure identity platform used to perform remote identity proofing for federal agencies and highly regulated environments. Identity is confirmed by correlating biometric signals, government-issued credentials, device attributes, and authoritative and credible evidence sources in real time. CLEAR1 protects high-stakes workflows such as onboarding, account creation and recovery, and sensitive data access. The platform is policy-driven and aligned to the NIST Digital Identity Guidelines, including IAL2 and AAL2, and is approved by the Kantara Initiative as meeting these standards.
