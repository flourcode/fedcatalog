What it does

Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) is a FedRAMP Moderate Software as a Service (SaaS) enabling the ability to create, manage, and deliver digital experiences across every channel, including web, mobile apps, digital forms and communities, and on-site screens. Forrester ranks Adobe as the leader in agile content management, receiving the highest scores possible for current offerings, strategy, and market presence. This SaaS package includes Adobe Experience Manager Sites, Adobe Experience Manager Assets, and Adobe Experience Manager Forms for web content management, digital asset management, digital enrollment and smart forms, app development and management, and social communities capabilities.
