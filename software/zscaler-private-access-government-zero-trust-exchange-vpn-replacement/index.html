What it does

Zscaler Private AccessGov, part of the Zscaler Zero Trust Exchange, enables fast, secure connections and allows your employees to work from anywhere using the internet as the corporate network. Based on the zero-trust principle of least-privileged access, it provides comprehensive security using context-based identity and policy enforcement. Zero Trust Exchange helps agencies achieve the target goals of the Executive Order 14028 and resulting OMB mandates, CISA guidance and directives. ZPA Gov security products are currently utilized by over 100 federal agencies and federal systems integrators today in Zscaler's US-based FedRAMP Moderate and FedRAMP JAB High approved boundaries in support of
