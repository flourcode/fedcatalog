What it does

Wiz for U.S. Government Wiz for U.S. Government (“Wiz for Gov”) is a cloud-native environment and product line that delivers a unified view of risk across a customer’s full cloud environment, from code to production. Wiz for Gov helps public sector agencies meet FedRAMP and FISMA compliance needs by focusing on the issues that matter most. The Wiz for Gov unified product line has three different components to secure your stack: Wiz Cloud for Gov Wiz Cloud focuses on assessing and prioritizing risk by analyzing vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, malware, identities, network paths, data, and AI risks.
