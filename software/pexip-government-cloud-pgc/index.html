What it does

Pexip Government Cloud is a SaaS-based video teleconferencing (VTC) solution for U.S. federal, state, and local government organizations. With Pexip, government customers can augment their existing on-premise VTC infrastructure with a subscription-based service model while retaining ownership of their specific endpoint
