What it does

RMA iQ™ is delivered as SaaS offering using a multi-tenant Government Only cloud computing environment. It is available to the DoD, federal, state, local and tribal government communities as well as their respective contractors. Feith RMA iQ is a SaaS platform designed to support federal agencies in managing records efficiently. It helps Records Managers address challenges such as large volumes of records, complex retention schedules, and compliance with federal records regulations. Key Features -Automated Classification and Retention: Employs artificial intelligence to classify records and apply GRS and RCS retention schedules, reducing manual effort and improving accuracy. -Efficient Recor
