What it does

Adobe Connect is a FedRAMP Moderate Software as a Service (SaaS) running on International Traffic and Arms Regulations (ITAR) compliant IT infrastructure, offering immersive online meeting experiences for real-time collaboration, large-scale webinars, and virtual classrooms in a secure environment. Go beyond screen sharing and web conferencing with customizable pods and layouts, polls, quizzes, drawing boards, simulations, breakout rooms, live video, screen sharing, chat, gamification, telephony integration, and persistent virtual rooms with strong access authentication.
