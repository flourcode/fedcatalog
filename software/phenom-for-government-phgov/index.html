What it does

Phenom is an applied AI company committed to helping public agencies hire faster, develop better, and retain longer - all through the power of experience and intelligence. Our AI-powered talent experience platform connects every stakeholder in the talent journey - candidates, recruiters, employees, and hiring managers - to improve productivity, retention, and hiring outcomes. Phenom for Government is delivered as a SaaS offering using a multi-tenant Government-only cloud computing environment. It is available to federal, state, local, and tribal governments, as well as research institutions, federal contractors, and government contractors.
