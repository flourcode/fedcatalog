What it does

911inform is a secure, cloud-based emergency response and situational intelligence platform designed for federal and enterprise environments requiring real-time visibility and coordinated response during critical incidents. Delivered within a FedRAMP-aligned SaaS architecture, the platform provides a centralized command-and-control interface for managing emergencies across facilities and agencies. At its core is 911inform’s patented Location Discovery Solution (LDS), a Ray Baum’s Act-compliant technology that delivers precise, dynamic location data for 911 calls across PBX, softphone, and mobile devices.
