What it does

Kovr AI is a patented AI-native cyber compliance automation platform purpose-built for organizations navigating the most demanding federal and commercial cybersecurity frameworks including FedRAMP, Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC 2.0), NIST SP 800-53, NIST SP 800-171, DoD Security Requirements Guide (SRG), FISMA, DoD Risk Management Framework (RMF), PCI-DSS, ISO 27001, and more.
