What it does

TTEC Digital’s Humanify Enterprise for Government (Humanify G) is a secure customer experience VOIP solution, powered by Cisco’s UCCE and UC Enterprise Software. Designed for agencies with up to 80,000 unified communications endpoints and 24,000 knowledge workers or agents, it allows you to scale as needed to meet citizen demand. By applying technologies like journey orchestration, analytics, and AI/automation you can deliver efficient and intuitive constituent services while reducing overall contact center costs.
