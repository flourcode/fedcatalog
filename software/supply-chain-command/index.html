What it does

Supply Chain Command (SCC) is a web based end-to-end, comprehensive solution that delivers quantifiable risk reduction across your entire third-party ecosystem, higher levels of efficiency to risk management processes, and greater visibility across your supply chain. SCC drives cyber risk reduction at every stage of the third-party lifecycle, from sourcing & selection, on through ongoing monitoring and reassessment. The highly configurable solution set includes multiple levels of continuous monitoring based on vendor risk levels, and point-in-time risk assessments to support procurement and M&A decision making.
