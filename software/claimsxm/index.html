What it does

ClaimsXM is a robust platform inclusive of claims processing software, program code, and business rules and logic to support the operation and management of Customer claims processing activities by authorized users with all data hosted in the Microsoft Azure Government Cloud with FedRAMP certification. This data-driven approach to streamlining administrative tasks is an innovative, rules-based solution that reduces excessive administrative healthcare costs, provider frustrations, and the manual challenges of claims administration.
