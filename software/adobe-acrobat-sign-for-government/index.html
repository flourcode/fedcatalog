What it does

Adobe Acrobat Sign for Government is a security-enhanced instance of Adobe's industry-leading Software as a Service (SaaS) application that lets agencies rapidly replace manual, paper-based approval and signature processes with automated, all-digital workflows. Adobe Acrobat Sign for Government makes employees more productive in the office or working remotely by letting them sign documents, request signatures, and track processes from web and mobile devices - as well as inside applications they use every day. Signers can sign on desktop or mobile devices with no downloads or signups required. Adobe Acrobat Sign for Government capabilities include webforms, batch signing, automated workflows
