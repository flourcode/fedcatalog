What it does

Okta for Government High service offering provides centralized identity and access management capabilities to customers who want to manage access across any application or device, whether they are on-premises in the customer’s office/data center or in the cloud. The Okta IDaaS platform is the primary application platform provided to customers. The IDaaS application provides several important features/capabilities, which are listed below. - Universal Directory: Okta Universal Directory provides a single view across all these groups with AD and LDAP directory integrations and out-of-the-box connections with HR systems, CSV files, and third-party IdPs.
