What it does

LambdaX is a Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM) platform operated by Cognitus, an IBM company. It supports the creation, negotiation, approval, execution, storage, management, and analysis of contractual agreements within a defined authorization boundary. The system provides centralized contract authoring, workflow automation, document management, AI-assisted processing, reporting, and analytics. LambdaX serves government agencies, contractors, and enterprise organizations that require controlled access to contract data and structured lifecycle governance.
