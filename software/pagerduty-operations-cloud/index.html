What it does

The PagerDuty Operations Cloud is a comprehensive platform that enables automated detection, diagnosis, and response to a wide range of events, including customer support alerts, suspicious or abnormal behavior, system disruptions, and performance-impacting incidents. By leveraging advanced event orchestration and intelligent aggregation features, PagerDuty enhances the signal-to-noise ratio, allowing organizations to prioritize critical alerts while minimizing distractions from less significant events.
