What it does

Appian Government Cloud (AGC) provides Appian’s process automation platform for high-impact workloads. AGC offers Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) and Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) functionality, which empowers agencies to design, automate, and optimize their operations to increase efficiency and productivity. It's an ideal solution for government agencies looking to streamline processes, particularly in the areas of critical acquisitions, case management, and logistics. Any agency can use Appian to orchestrate and automate complex processes to improve its performance and better serve constituent needs.
