Hi, I’m Mark.

I spent six years supporting Federal Partner Sales at AWS after sales roles at F5, Red Hat, Western Digital, and STEC. Before that I spent 20 years in the Marines.

I’ve been the seller, the partner guy, and the person on the government side trying to work out what we could actually buy and where.

Federal software information is scattered across FedRAMP, the DoD Cyber Exchange, GSA, USAspending and the cloud marketplaces, and none of them talk to each other. FedCatalog puts the useful parts in one place: what a product is, whether it’s authorized, where it runs, and the ways government can buy it.

I don’t rank vendors, sell placement, or decide which product is “best.” The facts come from public sources, and every page says where they came from.

There’s no company behind this. It’s just me. If something is wrong or missing, email me at mark@fedcatalog.com.
