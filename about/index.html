About FedCatalog

Federal software information is scattered all over the place. FedRAMP has one piece, DoD has another, GSA has another, and the marketplaces have their own version of the story.

I built FedCatalog to put the useful parts together in one place.

The goal is simple: help people find the right federal software, understand its status, and figure out the available ways to buy it.

Hi, I’m Mark.

I spent most of my career around federal technology, first in the Marine Corps and later in enterprise tech, including several years at AWS.

I kept seeing the same problem from both sides: government buyers had trouble figuring out what they could actually use and where to get it, while vendors and partners had trouble explaining all the different paths clearly.

FedCatalog is my attempt to make that mess a little easier to navigate.

I don’t rank vendors, sell placement, or decide which product is “best.” The facts come from public sources, and FedCatalog shows where they came from.

If something is wrong or missing, email me at mark@fedcatalog.com.
